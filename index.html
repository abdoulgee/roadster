  • Tap the left or right half to move one lane. Swipe left or right for repeated lane changes, including in the air.
  • Swipe up to jump; keep holding to hover and release to fall. Swipe down to slam.
  • Boost by holding the BOOST button or holding anywhere on the road; drag to steer while boosting.
  • Item and BOOST buttons sit together on the left.
  • Air powers flying, flips and boost. It refills on the ground and from tricks.
  • Streaks of flips, rolls, clears and near misses raise your score multiplier.
  • Air orbs charge Rocket mode. The golden star makes you a Starman.
